# Cold Playtest Script - Award Readiness

Use this script with 3-5 people who have not watched development. Do not coach
strategy during the run. The goal is to find first-contact confusion, not to
prove the game can be completed by someone who already understands it.

## Setup

- Build or open the current live preview URL.
- Start each tester from the bare URL, not a chapter hash.
- Ask permission before recording screen, audio, or notes tied to their name.
- Keep all tester names and contact details out of committed files.
- Stop the session if the tester asks to stop, seems uncomfortable, or reaches
  20 minutes.

## Tasks

1. Start from the live URL.
2. Choose the path you would naturally choose.
3. Complete the first playable objective.
4. Say what you think Nova is and what blocked you.
5. Rate confusion from 1-5, where 1 means clear and 5 means lost.
6. If time remains, replay Crisis Run once or continue the campaign until the
   next chapter transition.

## Observer Notes

Record these without correcting the tester mid-task:

- First action: Crisis Run, Learn Mode, settings, Codex, or something else.
- Time to first meaningful interaction.
- First phrase the tester uses to describe the goal.
- First point where they ask what to do next.
- Whether they notice power, cooling, chips, and network as requirements.
- Whether they understand that campaign chips affect Crisis Run inventory.
- Whether the finale reads as a payoff or as another dashboard.

## Post-Run Questions

- What did you think the game wanted you to do in the first minute?
- What did Nova mean to you by the end of the session?
- Which rule felt most unfair or unexplained?
- Which moment felt most like a real choice?
- Would you replay Crisis Run to improve the result? Why or why not?
- What one sentence would you use to explain the game to someone else?

## Pass Criteria

The game is award-submission ready only if most cold testers can:

- Reach the first playable objective without facilitator instruction.
- Explain Nova as the public-interest AI or city-recovery mission.
- Name at least one supply-chain tradeoff after playing.
- Understand why a blocked action is blocked.
- Complete or intentionally replay Crisis Run without getting stuck in UI.

## Evidence To Keep

- Anonymous notes per tester.
- Confusion rating and replay intent.
- Browser/device used.
- Any screenshot or timestamped clip of a blocking moment.
- A short fix list grouped as blocker, warning, or polish.
